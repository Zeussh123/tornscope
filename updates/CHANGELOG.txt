TORNSCOPE - CHANGELOG
(newest first - updated at each automated check-up)

=== v3.8 - 2026-10-24 (check-up #3) ===
NEW
 - site-modules/planner-position.js (+ test): the planner takes the player's position into account.
   Already flying -> first take-off = landing + 30 s minimum; flying out / abroad -> must fly back first.
   Stock on landing and the day plan are computed for the real take-off time.
 - updates/PLANNER-POSITION-SPEC.txt: how to wire it (new /api/me/travel endpoint + manual form + banner).

=== v3.7 - 2026-10-24 (check-up #2) ===
NEW
 - site-modules/stock-insights.js (+ test): depletion rate, restock delay/qty, sell-out time, restock time of day
   with confidence, and a "will it be in stock when I land" verdict - the data shown by tornw3b's stock card.
 - updates/STOCK-CARD-SPEC.txt: how to turn the site's one-line stock row into that card.

=== v3.6 - 2026-10-24 (check-up #1) ===
FIXED
 - Refresh flicker / scroll jump: the panel redrew every list with innerHTML once per second,
   which could throw the scroll back to the top (very visible in Torn PDA) and closed dropdowns.
   Lists are now patched card by card: a card is only replaced when its content changed, the
   scroll position is saved/restored, and a card holding the focus (aircraft dropdown) is never touched.
 - Version numbers were inconsistent (header 3.5, VERSION 3.4, console 3.4): all are 3.6 now.
NEW
 - "Attack" button on every target card and every War card (opens Torn's attack page).
 - Vector icons (no external image, works offline / in PDA) on the tabs, status chips
   (Traveling, Abroad, Hospital, Jail, Okay) and buttons (Add, Sync, Test alert, Travel, Profile, Open site).
 - Hover feedback on tabs, red style for attack buttons.
