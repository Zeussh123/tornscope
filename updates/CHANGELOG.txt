== v3.6 (2026-10-24) - Smooth refresh round ==
FIXED
 - Page jumping to the top every second: the cards were fully rebuilt each
   refresh, which collapsed the scroll area. Cards (Targets and War) are now
   patched in place; only a card whose content really changed is touched.
   Scroll position, hover and the open aircraft dropdown are preserved
   (no more global "skip refresh while a dropdown is open" workaround).
 - Version strings were out of sync (header 3.5 / VERSION 3.4 / console 3.4).
ADDED
 - Country flags on destinations (Targets and War lists).
 - Icons on tabs and main buttons.
 - updates/ folder with this changelog and FEATURES.txt.
